    What this is簡介

    An AW Design interactive map of Hong Kong's building control documents. The Buildings Ordinance and its regulations, practice notes, codes, circular letters and the related Lands, Planning and Fire Services documents are drawn as one connected network, so you can see what cites what and follow a subject across departments.

    AW Design 製作的香港建築管制文件互動地圖。《建築物條例》及其規例、作業備考、守則、通函，以及地政、規劃及消防的相關文件，繪成一個互相連接的網絡，讓讀者看清文件之間的引用關係，並可跨部門追蹤同一課題。

    Method製作方法

    • Solid lines () are citations found in the documents' own text. Each one carries the quoted sentence and page.
    • Dashed lines () are links suggested by AI. Each one was checked against both documents and given a written reason.
    • Summaries were written by AI from the full text of each document, then checked against it. Where no source text was available the summary is marked "Summary from title only" ( documents).
    • Topic areas (the coloured regions and domains) use the Buildings Department's own vocabulary. Topics are never drawn as lines.
    • 實線（ 條）是文件原文中明確的引用，每條均附原文引句及頁碼。
    • 虛線（ 條）是 AI 建議的關係，每條均已對照兩份文件核實，並附書面理由。
    • 摘要由 AI 根據每份文件全文撰寫，再以原文核對。未能取得原文的文件，摘要註明「摘要只根據標題撰寫」（共 份）。
    • 範疇及主題（彩色區域）採用屋宇署本身的分類用語，主題不以連線表示。

    Limits使用限制

    • This is a navigation aid, not legal advice.
    • Always read the official source. Every document links to it.
    • Some documents are superseded, repealed or duplicated. They are kept for reference and drawn faded, with a note naming the replacement. Some have no Chinese version; where the Chinese title or summary is ours, the panel says so.
    • Dates come from the documents themselves, or from the department's index where the document is undated.
    • 本圖只屬導航工具，並非法律意見。
    • 請以官方原文為準，每份文件均附官方連結。
    • 部分文件已被取代、廢除或與其他文件重複，現予保留以供參考，並以淡色顯示及註明取代文件。部分文件沒有中文版；如中文標題或摘要由本站撰寫，資料欄會加以說明。
    • 日期取自文件本身；如文件未有註明日期，則取自部門索引。
